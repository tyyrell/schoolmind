Compliance overview

Compliance cannot be claimed casually.

SchoolMind AI is being structured around privacy, consent, retention, access control, auditability, and human supervision. Formal compliance claims require legal review for each market and deployment.

What is built in

Role-based access, CSRF protection, security headers, audit events, consent records, retention settings, exports, and school-admin controls.

What schools must define

Guardian consent, internal policy, staff permissions, local student privacy requirements, retention period, and escalation protocol.

What needs external review

Country-specific legal compliance, data processing agreement, subprocessors, incident response, and commercial terms.
