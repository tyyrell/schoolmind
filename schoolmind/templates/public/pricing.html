What happens after the trial?

No surprise conversion. The school chooses the billing path.

At the end of the 30-day trial, the workspace should move to monthly billing, annual billing, a guided pilot agreement, or pause until the school is ready.

  • Checkout must be completed through the configured payment provider before paid activation.
  • Manual activation stays locked for normal school admins by default.
  • Extra student charges should be approved before being billed.
  • Custom plans are for large schools, districts, data residency needs, or negotiated rollout support.
Billing guardrails

This is not a fake-free funnel.

Trial means evaluation

The free path is a 30-day trial to evaluate SchoolMind AI, not a permanent free plan and not permission to collect uncontrolled student data.

Checkout before activation

Paid subscriptions should activate through provider checkout and webhook confirmation, not by a school admin clicking a manual switch.

Pilot for serious rollouts

Schools that need onboarding, privacy review, or staff workflow design should request a guided pilot instead of self-serving a production rollout.
