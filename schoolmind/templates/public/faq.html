FAQ

Hard questions schools should ask before using any student support platform.

Does SchoolMind AI diagnose students?

No. It provides educational wellbeing indicators and workflow support for human-supervised school review. It is not a medical, diagnostic, emergency, or therapy service.

Can visitors try the product before signup?

Yes. Limited demo mode opens immediately. Production data, imports, billing, exports, and sensitive operations require a real workspace.

Is English the default language?

Yes. English is the default, and Arabic support is available with RTL layout support. Additional languages can be expanded later.

How does the 30-day trial work?

A school can evaluate the platform for 30 days. Real student data should only be used after the school reviews consent, privacy, and internal governance requirements.

What happens after the included student limit?

Standard and Pro include student limits. Larger volumes can move to custom terms or negotiated extra-student pricing.
