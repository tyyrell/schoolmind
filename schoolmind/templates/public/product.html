Product overview

One supervised operating system for school support.

SchoolMind AI brings students, teachers, counselors, and school leaders into a governed workflow for educational wellbeing indicators, support follow-up, consent, and operational readiness.

Platform architecture

Explore first. Register when the school needs real data and governance.

Visitors can enter a limited sandbox immediately. Production use begins only when a school creates a workspace, reviews privacy requirements, invites staff, imports users, and connects billing.

  • Instant role-based demo for students, teachers, counselors, and admins.
  • Workspace setup for school profile, roles, consent, support owner, and data retention.
  • Privacy-aware data boundaries between private reflection, counselor workflow, and aggregate classroom insight.
  • Subscription controls for Standard, Pro, and custom school deployments.
