Features

Feature depth that supports a real school workflow.

The product is organized around the actions a school must actually run: explore, configure, collect signals, review safely, coordinate support, report responsibly, and manage operations.

Instant limited demo

Visitors enter a role-based sandbox immediately and see reminders when they reach locked production actions.

30-day trial

Schools can evaluate Standard or Pro before payment while keeping production data governance explicit.

Bilingual foundation

English is default, Arabic is supported with RTL layout, and the language switcher preserves the current page.

Student support tools

Check-ins, assessment, journal, goals, mood diary, daily tips, progress, and support requests.

Nour AI-assisted companion

Educational support prompts and safe routing language with clear human-supervision boundaries.

Teacher aggregate pulse

Class-level insight that avoids exposing private journals or counselor-only notes.

Counselor workflow

Support queues, student detail views, playbooks, intervention actions, and support plans.

Admin operations

Invites, imports, consent, billing, plan limits, backups, database readiness, and security controls.

Audit and export

Workspace exports and audit events help schools understand operational activity and prepare for governance review.

Feature discipline

Good features have limits.

SchoolMind AI must be useful without pretending to be a clinician, lawyer, or emergency service. That boundary is part of the product, not a weakness.
