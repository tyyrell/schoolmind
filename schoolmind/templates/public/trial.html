Trial rules

What must be clear before real student data enters the workspace.

Human review required

SchoolMind AI provides educational indicators and workflow support. It does not make final decisions, diagnose students, or replace school staff judgment.

Consent and governance first

Schools should review guardian consent, student notices, staff roles, and retention settings before using production student records.

No fake free plan

The free path is a 30-day trial. After that, the school should move to monthly billing, annual billing, a guided pilot agreement, or pause usage.
