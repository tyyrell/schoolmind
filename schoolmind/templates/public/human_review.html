Human review

School staff stay responsible for support decisions.

SchoolMind AI can organize educational indicators and support workflows, but it must not replace qualified human judgment, school policy, guardian communication, or emergency procedures.

Operating principle

The platform can assist. The school must decide.

  • AI-assisted outputs are framed as educational support suggestions, not diagnoses.
  • Serious signals are routed toward authorized human review instead of automated conclusions.
  • Counselors and school leaders define follow-up, escalation, and documentation practices.
  • Students should understand when their school may review support requests or safety-related signals.
