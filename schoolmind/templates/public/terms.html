Terms

Use SchoolMind AI as a supervised school support platform.

These terms define the product posture: educational indicators, school governance, human review, and responsible trial use. They are not final commercial legal terms for every country.

{{ legal_notice }}

Educational support only

The platform provides educational wellbeing indicators and workflow support. It does not provide medical diagnosis, clinical treatment, emergency response, or legal advice.

School responsibility

Schools are responsible for user access, guardian consent where required, local policy, staff training, data ownership, and human support decisions.

Trial use

The 30-day trial is for evaluation. Production student data should not be added until privacy, consent, and deployment readiness are reviewed.

Billing

Standard and Pro plans are priced per school. Annual billing can reduce the effective monthly cost. Larger deployments may use negotiated terms.

Acceptable use

Do not use the platform to harass, profile, discriminate, bypass school policy, or make unsupported conclusions about a student.

Emergency boundary

SchoolMind AI is not an emergency service. Schools must use their approved emergency and safeguarding procedures outside the platform.

Commercial readiness

Do not sign real schools on vague promises.

Before paid production use, the operator should publish final subprocessors, connect production database/email/payment systems, define support terms, and review privacy obligations.

Related documents

Read the privacy policy, DPA draft, student data notice, retention policy, and incident response overview together. One page alone is not enough for a school product.
