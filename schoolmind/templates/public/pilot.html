Guided pilot

Use a controlled pilot before moving serious school operations into production.

A pilot is for schools that need onboarding, privacy review, staff workflow design, launch governance, or multi-campus planning instead of only a self-serve trial.

Pilot is not just free access.

A serious school pilot should define scope, data boundaries, success criteria, staff responsibilities, and the decision point for monthly, annual, custom, or paused rollout.

  • Best for 1,000+ students or complex staff teams.
  • Best when privacy or legal review is required.
  • Best when the school wants launch support instead of guessing.
