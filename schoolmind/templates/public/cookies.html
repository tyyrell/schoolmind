Cookie policy

Minimal cookies for a safer school product.

SchoolMind AI should use only necessary cookies unless a school explicitly approves analytics or marketing tools. This draft keeps the default posture conservative.

Required session cookie

Used to keep users signed in, protect CSRF flow, preserve language/theme preferences, and keep the limited demo working.

Preference state

Language, theme, font size, contrast, reduced motion, and demo reminders may be stored in the session or user preferences.

No default ad tracking

Do not add advertising pixels or invasive analytics to a student support platform without written school approval.
