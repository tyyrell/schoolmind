About SchoolMind AI

A global school support platform with Arabic and English access.

SchoolMind AI is designed for schools that need clearer wellbeing indicators, safer support workflows, and practical dashboards without turning software into a clinical authority.

The product should be presented honestly: pilot-ready, human-supervised, and built for responsible school evaluation before full production use.

Mission

Help schools notice educational wellbeing patterns earlier and coordinate support more clearly.

Boundary

SchoolMind AI does not diagnose, treat, or replace professional judgment, guardians, school counselors, or emergency response.

Direction

English is the default language, Arabic is supported, and the platform is intended for global school environments.
